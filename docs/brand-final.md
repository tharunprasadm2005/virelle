# Virelle — final brand record (formerly TasteFlow)

- **Name:** Virelle (veer-RELL). Invented brand name; meaning is brand-created.
- **Story/positioning:** the companion that learns your taste and answers
  "what do I cook?" from what you like, what you have, and what you want now.
- **Tagline (kept):** "Recipes that learn your taste."
- **Logo:** "The Converging Drop" — seed-strokes fusing into one golden drop
  (`frontend/assets/virelle-mark.svg`, `favicon.svg`); wordmark + mark in all
  navbars; React copies under `frontend-react/public/assets/`.
- **Palette (exact):** Deep Brown #5A4A0D (text/icon base) · Dark Gold #7A5E12
  (borders) · Warm Gold #A67917 (secondary) · Amber Gold #CC961F (primary
  actions) · Main Gold #E8B84A (logo/drop/highlights) · Light Gold #F3D789
  (soft surfaces) · Cream #FFF3D8 (background).
- **Typography:** display serif for logo/hero; system stack for UI (readability,
  zero licensing friction). No ornamental body text.
- **Favicon:** drop mark on deep brown, all pages (classic + React).
- **Availability:** web screening found no obvious active collisions (see
  `docs/brand-research.md`); this is NOT legal clearance — engage counsel
  before commercial launch.
- **Kept identifiers (deliberate, do not "fix"):** `tasteflow.db` filename,
  `TASTEFLOW_SECRET` / `TASTEFLOW_DB` env vars, `tasteflow-demo` password,
  logger name, migration-history strings, research-doc history. Renaming any of
  these breaks existing installs, logins, or audit trails for zero user benefit.
- **Usage rules:** product surfaces say Virelle; research/academic contexts keep
  the full academic title; never present the drop mark next to generic food
  clip-art; monochrome version = solid ink drop.
