# Brand research (pre-rename — DO NOT rename yet; awaiting user approval)

> Trademark screening here is NOT legal advice and NOT legal clearance.
> A full attorney-led clearance search is required before commercial launch.

## 1. Current name problem
"TasteFlow" is descriptive (taste + flow), hard to own, and generic-AI-startup
sounding. It describes the mechanism, not the feeling. The product needs a
short, ownable, premium food/lifestyle word that can grow beyond recipes.

## 2. Positioning
"Know what to cook." A premium companion that learns taste over time and turns
pantry + preferences into tonight's dinner, the week's plan, and the list.

## 3. Blacklist (never reuse, incl. trivial variants)
TasteFlow, FounDesk, Transiq, Vellora, Annova, Prevora, Kinetix, Holst,
Savorin, Velora, Mavora, Orvella, Savelle, Aureli (+ AI/Kitchen/Food/App combos).

## 4. 50-name exploration (internal; screened for pronounceability/meaning/tone)

Nourra, Emberly, Hearthly, Sagra, Rasa, Zaika, Swad, Tadka, Handi, Dumaya, Sigdi,

## 5. Eliminated + reason
- Generic-word collisions: Cocina, Tavola, Mesa, Umami, Aroma (all heavily used).
- Active food-brand collisions: Savoria/Savora (Savora pantry+planner apps, App
  Store, direct competitor — RED), Zestora (Play Store restaurant app — RED),
  Zaffran (restaurants + Zaffron restaurant-tech + grocery app — RED),
  Elaichi (restaurants + food-marketplace app — RED), Emberly (recipe blog),
  Hearthly (recipe site + home apps), Sagra (restaurants + equipment co.),
  Rasa/Zaika/Swad/Tadka/Handi (dozens of restaurants each).
- Near-miss corporate: Gustora/Gustava (≈ Gusto payroll), Festora (≈ Festool),
  Vantara (Ambani animal project), Nourra (≈ Noura nutrition apps).
- Weak/descriptive: Simmerly, Forkful, Skilletly, Palatta.
- Palatia: dropped late — Palate/Palato/PalAte app cluster (incl. Palato recipe +
  meal-plan app) crowds the phonetic space. RED by proximity.
- Dumaya: YELLOW only (Dumyah shopping app, different spelling) — runner-up.

## 6-11. Final 5 + availability/collision/domain/GitHub/app-store findings
Research method: web search (brand+app+food queries), App Store/Play signals,
GitHub-adjacent results. No active product/brand collisions discovered for any
of the five (screenshots/notes in session log 2026-10-07). Domains/GitHub orgs
not reserved by us — check at selection time; availability changes fast.

### F1. Kesarra (keh-SAH-rrah)
- Invented spelling; "kesar" is genuinely Hindi/Marathi for saffron.
- Story: the world's most precious ingredient — a little transforms everything;
  like the product does with taste knowledge. Saffron == gold palette directly.
- Personality: warm, premium, exotic-but-pronounceable, culinary, personal.
- Taglines: "Know what to cook." / "A little taste goes a long way." /
  "Your kitchen, golden."
- Scores: Distinct 9, Memor 8, Pron 8, Spell 7, Food-fit 9, Premium 10, Global 8,
  Logo 9, Domain 8, No-collision 9 → **8.6**

### F2. Morsela (mor-SELL-ah)
- From English "morsel" (a small bite) + invented -a ending. Meaning brand-created.
- Story: great cooking is small joys — the right bite, tonight. Soft, friendly,
  international; zero food-brand baggage found.
- Personality: warm, gentle, modern, personal, calm, welcoming.
- Taglines: "Tonight, decided." / "Small bites, big taste." / "What to cook, known."
- Scores: 8,9,9,8,7,8,9,8,8,9 → **8.4**

### F3. Imliya (im-LEE-yah)
- From "imli" (tamarind, Hindi) + -ya; tangy, lively, distinctive.
- Story: the sour spark that wakes a dish up — the app that wakes up dinner.
  Gold-amber tamarind pulp fits the palette story.
- Personality: lively, warm, distinctive, youthful, natural.
- Taglines: "Wake up dinner." / "Know what to cook." / "Tangy little genius."
- Scores: 9,8,7,7,8,8,7,9,8,9 → **8.3**

### F4. Garama (gah-RAH-mah)
- From "garam" (warm/hot, Hindi/Urdu) + -a; invented brand name.
- Story: warm food, warm home, warm gold. The comfort positioning made ownable.
- Personality: warm, comforting, homely, calm, trustworthy.
- Taglines: "Warm food, decided." / "Your taste, served warm." / "Know what to cook."
- Scores: 8,8,8,8,8,7,8,8,8,9 → **8.1**

### F5. Methika (meh-THEE-kah)
- From "methi" (fenugreek) + -ka; earthy, uncommon, easy to say.
- Story: the quiet background note that makes the dish — like taste knowledge
  working invisibly. Slightly more niche feel.
- Personality: earthy, natural, calm, understated, premium-niche.
- Taglines: "The quiet art of dinner." / "Know what to cook." / "Taste, understood."
- Scores: 8,7,8,8,7,8,8,8,8,9 → **7.9**

Runners-up: Cravara 7.6 (docked: Craver restaurant-SaaS phonetic neighbor),
Dumaya 7.2 (Dumyah shopping app neighbor).

## 12-14. Recommendation + color + type
**Recommended: Kesarra** — highest score, saffron→gold story fuses name and
palette, premium and ownable, no active collisions found.

Color system (from supplied palette):
- Primary #CC961F · Deep text/ink #5A4A0D · Dark gold (borders/patriarch) #7A5E12
- Amber accents #E8B84A · Soft gold surfaces #F3D789 · Cream bg #FFF3D8
- Olive gold #A67917 for secondary marks · Success sage (existing green) ·
  warning/error keep current rust/red.
Typography: display — a high-contrast serif with premium feel (e.g. Fraunces,
OFL-licensed) for logo/hero only; body/UI — Inter or system stack (readability
+ zero licensing friction). Final font choice at migration time.

## 15. Logo directions (for approved name)
1. Symbol + wordmark: abstract mark of 3–4 seed/petal strokes converging into a
   single golden drop (ingredients → one meal), wordmark in display serif.
2. Minimal mark: single continuous-line spiral-bowl in primary gold.
3. App icon/favicon: gold rounded square, cream drop + deep-ink initial letter.
All must read on cream, deep-ink, gold, and pure monochrome.
